Ensemble Covariance & Localization

Why finite ensembles produce spurious correlations — and how the Schur product fixes them

The Sampling Problem

B̂ens = 1/(N−1) ∑i (xi − x̄)(xi − x̄)T
With N ensemble members, the sample covariance B̂ens is a noisy estimate of the true B. Finite sampling creates spurious long-range correlations — grid points that appear correlated when they shouldn't be. Smaller ensemble → more noise.

The Localization Fix (Schur Product)

Beff = L ∘ B̂ens
L is a smooth, positive-definite localization matrix that tapers to zero at distance. The Schur (element-wise) product zeros out spurious remote correlations while preserving nearby structure.
Gaussian: L(d) = exp(−d²/2L²)   Gaspari-Cohn: compact support (exact zero beyond 2L)
